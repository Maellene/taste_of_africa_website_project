Jollof Rice

Jollof is a celebrated one-pot rice dish from West Africa. Nigeria and Ghana playfully compete over who makes it best. Its flavor comes from tomatoes, scotch bonnet and thyme.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste

Instructions

Step 1: Make the sauce

Blend tomatoes, onion and pepper, then fry with tomato paste until thick.

Step 2: Cook the rice

Add washed rice and stock, cover, and simmer on low heat for 30 minutes.

Step 3: Serve

Fluff with a fork and serve with fried plantain.
