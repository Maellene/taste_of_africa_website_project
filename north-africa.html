North Africa

North African cooking mixes Arab, Berber and Mediterranean influences into fragrant, slow-cooked meals.

Staples

Key ingredients include couscous, olives, dates and preserved lemons, seasoned with cumin, harissa and saffron.

Cooking techniques

Dishes are often prepared by slow cooking in clay pots and steaming couscous over a stew.

Example dish

  • Tagine (Morocco)
