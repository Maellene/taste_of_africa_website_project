East Africa

East African cuisine ranges from spiced stews to hearty vegetable dishes, often served with a starchy side.

Staples

Everyday foods include cassava, teff, beans, maize and plantain, with peanut and berbere spice adding flavor.

Cooking techniques

Cooks rely on steaming and stewing, pounding leaves and long, gentle simmering.

Example dish

  • Isombe (Rwanda)
