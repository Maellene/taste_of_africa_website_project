West Africa

West African food culture is built on hearty stews and one-pot meals. Staples include rice, yam and plantain. Common techniques are slow simmering and open-fire grilling.

Example dishes

  • Jollof Rice

North Africa

North African cooking mixes Arab, Berber and Mediterranean influences. Staples include couscous, olives and dates. Flavors come from cumin, harissa and preserved lemons, with slow cooking in clay pots.

Example dishes

  • Tagine (Morocco)
  • Couscous (Algeria, Tunisia, Morocco)

East Africa

East African cuisine ranges from Ethiopian spiced stews to Rwandan vegetable dishes. Staples include teff, cassava and beans. Signature flavors include berbere spice, and cooking often involves steaming and stewing.

Example dishes

  • Injera
  • Isombe
