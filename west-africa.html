West Africa

West African food culture is built on hearty stews and one-pot meals shared with family and friends.

Staples

Everyday cooking centers on rice, yam, plantain and cassava, paired with tomato and pepper sauces.

Cooking techniques

Common methods include slow simmering, frying in palm oil and open-fire grilling.

Example dish

  • Jollof Rice (Nigeria)
